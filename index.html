What is ParaSwap

What ParaSwap is

A DEX aggregator

ParaSwap is a DEX aggregator. Instead of trading against a single pool, it searches many liquidity sources and routes an order across them to find a competitive price. It works on several EVM chains, so the same swap flow applies wherever your tokens are.

Who it is for

Anyone swapping ERC-20 tokens who would rather not compare DEXs by hand. A large order can be split across venues to reduce price impact, and a small one can follow the cheapest path once gas is counted.

Aggregator vs DEX

Aggregator vs a single DEX

A DEX is one venue with its own pools. An aggregator sits on top of many venues and decides where each part of your order goes.

One DEX vs an aggregator route

In practice

The aggregator quote already accounts for gas on each route, so the cheapest-looking path is not always the one with the most hops. You see the final expected output before you sign.

How to swap

How to swap with ParaSwap

Route and quote

After you pick a pair and an amount, the aggregator compares routes and shows the expected output, price impact and a slippage tolerance. Nothing is signed until you confirm.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and select the network you want to trade on.
  2. Choose the token to sell and the token to buy, then enter an amount.
  3. Review the quote, the route and the estimated gas.
  4. Approve the sell token once if this wallet has not used it before.
  5. Sign the swap; it settles on-chain and the bought token arrives in your wallet.

Native coins such as ETH need no approval, only the swap transaction.

Fees & gas

What a swap costs

Gas

Approving and swapping are separate transactions, each paid in the native coin of the chain you use. See how gas works.

Trading cost

The quoted output already reflects pool fees, price impact and any aggregator fee. Your slippage tolerance sets how far the final price may move before the transaction reverts.

Keep native coin for gas

Holding stablecoins or other tokens does not pay for gas. Keep a little of the chain's native coin in the wallet, or you cannot approve or sign.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface quotes and routes; it never holds your funds or private keys.

Before you sign

  • Check the token contract address; lookalike tokens are common.
  • Approve only the amount you need and revoke old ERC-20 allowances you no longer use.
  • Compare price impact and slippage with the size of your order.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

ParaSwap FAQ

What is ParaSwap used for?

ParaSwap is used to swap one token for another through a DEX aggregator. It searches several liquidity sources and routes your order across them, so you get one quote instead of checking each exchange yourself.

How does an aggregator find a price?

It queries many pools and venues, then tests single and split routes, including multi-hop paths. The route with the strongest expected output after gas is the one shown in the quote.

Why does my quote change before I confirm?

Pool balances and gas costs move with every block. Slippage tolerance caps how far the price may drift, and if the market moves past it the transaction reverts instead of filling at a worse rate.

Do I need to approve a token before swapping?

For ERC-20 tokens, yes. A one-time allowance lets the router move that token on your behalf, and it is a separate transaction that costs gas. Native coins such as ETH need no approval.

Which networks can I use?

The aggregator works on several EVM chains. Pick the network in your wallet and the interface, and trade tokens that exist on that chain. Moving tokens between chains needs a bridge, not a swap.

Does this interface hold my funds?

No. It is non-custodial: you connect your own wallet and sign each transaction, and tokens move directly between your wallet and the on-chain contracts. There is no account to create and no deposit to make.
